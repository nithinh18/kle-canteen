Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Common queries about digital pre-orders, token pickups, and payment modes.

How does the digital queue / token system work?

When you order through the website, you receive a unique digital token (e.g. #KLE-142) and counter assignment. Simply walk to the counter once the live countdown is complete, show your digital token or QR slip, and pick up your hot food immediately.

Can I pay using UPI (GPay/PhonePe/Paytm)?

Yes! When placing an order, a live UPI QR code is automatically generated for your exact total in Rupees (₹). You can also choose Cash at Counter or tap your KLE Student Smart Card.

How do I claim the 15% KLE Student Discount?

Use the coupon code KLESTUDENT in your cart drawer. You can also show your valid KLE University ID card at the pickup counter for verification.

Can I pre-order bulk combos for student club events or fests?

Yes! We cater to all IEEE, ACM, Cultural, and Sports fest events on campus. Contact the Canteen Manager directly at the counter or via helpline for fest discounts.
